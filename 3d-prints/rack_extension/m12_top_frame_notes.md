# M-12 evidence notes (top-frame obstruction survey)

Date reviewed: 2026-09-16. Source: user-supplied top-down photo of the
populated T1 (photo file itself not yet committed; see below).

## Observations

- Visible top-frame seating areas (left/right members, front/rear
  crossmembers) appear broadly flat. No protruding screw heads, lips, or
  other obstacles are visible on the main mounting areas.
- A transparent ruler laid across the top spans roughly 0-28 cm edge to
  edge, qualitatively corroborating the 281 mm external width. Parallax
  (ruler floating above the frame, oblique camera angle) means this is
  corroboration only, not a measurement.
- Rack-hole columns are visible on the side members, consistent with the
  recorded hole pattern. No hardware occupies the corner attachment zones
  (Y = 25/38/157/170 regions) in the photo.

## Limitations

- Ethernet cables cross the frame, so the seating surface is not fully
  visible in one shot.
- Oblique angle: coplanarity/flatness cannot be proven from this photo;
  the 2-4 mm registration lip design must still tolerate minor surface
  variation, and final seating is proven by the Phase 1 coupon fit test.
