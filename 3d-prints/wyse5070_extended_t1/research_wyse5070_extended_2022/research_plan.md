# Dell Wyse 5070 Extended 2022 chassis research

## Main question

Which externally verifiable dimensions, vent locations, foot geometry, and
chassis details should constrain a DeskPi RackMate T1 cradle for a 2022 Dell
Wyse 5070 Extended?

## Subtopics

1. Dell primary documentation: official dimensions, chassis variants, product
   orientation, and drawings or photographs.
2. Exterior geometry: clear side, bottom, front, and rear photographs that can
   confirm vents, feet, bezel alignment, corners, and PCIe-side asymmetry.
3. Existing CAD and mounts: dimensioned third-party models or rack mounts that
   reveal fit clearances, retention strategies, and Extended-versus-standard
   differences.

## Synthesis

Prefer Dell documentation for nominal dimensions, use independent photographs
for qualitative geometry, and use the user's physical measurements as the
source of truth where published data conflicts. Only measurements with a clear
scale or dimensioned drawing will be promoted into CAD parameters.
